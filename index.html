Project Overview

A centralized system for a growing multi-branch business

IGLÚ is a self-service frozen yogurt and ice cream business expanding across multiple branches in Barangay Salapungan, Angeles City. As operations grow, separately-kept employee, attendance, and cashier records create inconsistency, delays, and security risk. IgluHub is proposed as a web-based Multi-Branch Store Management Information System to centralize these records under one secure, role-based platform.

Who it's forMulti-branch retail

Built for IGLÚ's branch owners, head office management, and branch employees who need a single source of truth across locations.

What it centralizesEmployees · Attendance · Sales

Employee records, time-in/time-out attendance, and cashier transactions — unified in one centralized, validated database.

How it protects dataRoles · Audit trails

Role-based access controls and audit logging reduce unauthorized changes to sales and attendance records and improve accountability.

Background & Problem Statement

Why IgluHub is needed

Background of the StudyDisconnected records across branches

Businesses operating across multiple branches need organized systems to manage employee information, attendance, sales, and daily operations. In multi-branch store operations, managing employee records, attendance, and cashier transactions separately may lead to data inconsistencies, delays in updating information, and difficulties monitoring branch performance. Manual recordkeeping and disconnected digital files also limit accessibility and increase the risk of unauthorized modifications to important records.

Statement of the ProblemWhat the project addresses

The project aims to address difficulties in managing employee records, attendance, cashier-related information, and branch operations when records are maintained separately — practices that may contribute to delays, inconsistent information, difficulty monitoring activities, and exposure of sales/attendance records to unauthorized modification.

Specific Problems the Project Seeks to Answer

  • Employee Records Management How can employee information be organized and managed efficiently across different branches?
  • Attendance Monitoring How can employee attendance be recorded and monitored consistently across branches?
  • Cashier Operations Monitoring How can cashier-related records and sales transactions be organized and monitored more efficiently?
  • Branch Monitoring How can authorized users access and monitor branch information through a centralized system?
  • Data Consistency How can the consistency and timely updating of records across different branches be improved?
  • Unauthorized Data Modification How can the system help prevent and detect unauthorized changes to sales and attendance records?
Project Objectives

General & specific objectives

General Objective

To develop IgluHub, an integrated multi-branch Store Management Information System that provides a centralized, organized, and secure platform for managing employee records, attendance, and cashier operations across different branches.

Specific Objectives

  1. To develop a centralized employee record management feature that allows authorized personnel to efficiently add, update, view, and manage employee information across different branches.
  2. To develop an attendance management feature that allows authorized personnel to record, monitor, and review employee attendance records, including time-in, time-out, absences, and tardiness.
  3. To develop a cashier management feature that allows authorized personnel to record, organize, and monitor cashier-related transactions and sales records.
  4. To provide a centralized platform that allows authorized management personnel to access and monitor employee, attendance, and cashier information from different branches in one system.
  5. To implement centralized data storage and validation mechanisms to minimize inconsistent, duplicated, delayed, or missing information between branches.
  6. To implement user access controls and audit trail mechanisms that restrict unauthorized changes and record user activities involving sales and attendance data, thereby improving accountability and facilitating review of suspicious or unauthorized modifications.

Scope & Limitations

What IgluHub covers — and what it doesn't

The project covers the design, development, and testing of IgluHub for participating IGLÚ branches in Barangay Salapungan, Angeles City, using a centralized database and role-based access.

In Scope

  • Employee records: create, view, update, search, assign to a branch, activate/deactivate accounts.
  • Attendance: record time-in/time-out, view history, review absences and tardiness.
  • Cashier transactions: multi-item sales, product/weight/price/discount/GCash reference entry, totals and change.
  • Transaction review & correction: manager authorization for sensitive edits/voids, logged rather than overwritten.
  • Branch management: branch info, employee assignment, permitted report filtering by branch/date.
  • Dashboard & reports: summaries of sales, transaction counts, attendance, payment methods, product performance.
  • Security & accountability: role-based permissions, input validation, session controls, audit logging.

Limitations

  • Requires internet access for normal use and sync; offline processing is not included unless separately approved.
  • Accuracy depends on correct data entry, verified prices, and business rules supplied by IGLÚ.
  • Records cash/GCash payment details but does not directly connect to or authorize bank/e-wallet payments.
  • Excludes payroll computation, inventory purchasing, supplier management, accounting, loyalty accounts, and automated tax filing unless later approved.
  • Security reduces risk but cannot guarantee prevention of all unauthorized activity or data loss.
  • Performance depends on chosen hosting/database, branch devices, and internet quality.
  • Pilot testing is limited to branches, users, devices, and periods approved by IGLÚ and the adviser.

Possible Risks & Proposed Solutions

Risk management plan

Possible RiskProposed Solution
Inaccurate or incomplete system requirements — the team may misunderstand IGLÚ's actual processes, resulting in mismatched features.Conduct structured consultations with IGLÚ representatives before development; document confirmed business/pricing/discount rules, employee assignments, and transaction procedures; validate requirement summaries before design and development.
Scope creep — requests like payroll, inventory purchasing, accounting, or payment gateway integration may extend the timeline beyond team capacity.Maintain the approved scope and limitations document; the Scope & Communication Coordinator records change requests for team/adviser review; out-of-scope features are documented as future enhancements.
Schedule delays from simultaneous academic and personal responsibilities.Use ClickUp to assign tasks, set deadlines, and monitor progress; divide work by role; hold regular short progress meetings; identify delayed tasks early.
Technical difficulties implementing role-based access, centralized storage, calculations, audit logging, or responsive design.Early technical planning with VS Code, GitHub, and suitable DB tools; develop core features first, test incrementally, use version control to track changes and recover from errors.
Internet connectivity issues during development, testing, or actual use.Inform IGLÚ that stable internet is required for normal access; use backup connections during testing; documentation will state offline processing is out of scope.
Data entry errors in employee, attendance, product, price, or transaction records.Input validation for required fields/formats/values; confirmation screens before submission; authorized review of records; test data used during development instead of real data unless authorized.
Unauthorized access or modification of sensitive records.Secure login, role-based permissions, session controls; restrict transaction edits/voids to authorized managers; maintain audit log recording user, action, timestamp, affected record, and reason.
Loss of project or system data from accidental deletion, system errors, or hosting/database issues.GitHub for source-code version control and organized document backups; use hosting/DB provider backup capabilities; document a basic recovery procedure before pilot testing.
Limited budget or unexpected expenses beyond the preliminary estimate.Prioritize free tools and free-tier services; record expenses and retain receipts; PM and Technical Planning & Risk Coordinator review expenses before purchase; contingency fund for documented needs only.
Free hosting/database services may not meet the system's needs (storage, performance, downtime, security).Evaluate free-tier services against storage, reliability, security, backup, and pilot-testing needs before deployment; prepare updated cost estimates and seek approval if inadequate.
Low user adoption or difficulty using the system among IGLÚ staff.Design a simple, responsive interface; provide role-appropriate accounts; conduct a brief orientation/demo during pilot testing; gather feedback for improvements.
Device or browser compatibility issues on IGLÚ staff computers/smartphones.Test using common desktop and mobile browsers and available device types; address major display/functionality issues before pilot; document supported browsers/devices.
Unavailable client representatives or delayed feedback.Coordinate schedules early via the Scope & Communication Coordinator; set consultation/feedback deadlines; prepare specific questions/demos; document confirmed decisions.
Insufficient testing before deployment — undetected errors in attendance, computation, discounts, payments, or permissions.Prepare test accounts/records/products/transactions; conduct functional testing for each major feature; document results and resolve defects before pilot implementation.

Conclusion

A practical, sustainable information management solution

The proposed IgluHub: A Web-Based Multi-Branch Store Management Information System for IGLÚ in Barangay Salapungan, Angeles City aims to address the challenges associated with managing employee records, attendance, cashier transactions, and branch operations through a centralized and organized platform. By integrating these essential business functions into one system, the project seeks to improve the accessibility, consistency, and accuracy of operational information.

IgluHub aims to strengthen operational efficiency, data security, and accountability through its centralized database, role-based access controls, audit logs, dashboards, and reporting features — supporting consistent recordkeeping, monitoring of sales and employee performance, and informed management decision-making.

Its successful implementation will depend on proper system development, comprehensive testing, accurate data entry, reliable internet connectivity, appropriate security measures, and active cooperation between the project team and business representatives. In the long term, the project may serve as a foundation for continuous operational improvement and help prepare IGLÚ for potential business expansion.
